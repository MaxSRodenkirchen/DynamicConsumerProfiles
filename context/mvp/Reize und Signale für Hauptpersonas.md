*Was in Studien berücksichtigt werden sollte:*
"Studies on consumer acceptance of cultured meat must provide participants with sufficient information about the product to ensure that their responses are based on a sound understanding of what cultured meat is. This includes essential details about its **pricing, taste, production process, healthiness, safety, environmental impact and ethical considerations**. Such information is particularly crucial when studying radical innovations, since the inherent novelty of these products often means that participants otherwise lack the necessary background knowledge to develop an informed opinion. " [^1]
