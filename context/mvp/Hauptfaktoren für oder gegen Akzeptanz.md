#### Persönliche Faktoren
- demografische und psychologische Faktoren
	- *Demografische sind auch wichtig aber schwieriger einzuordnen*
- **Emotionen**, besonders das Gefühl von Ekel [^7]
	- "a reduction in disgust has been associated with increased willingness to try cultured meat."
- "**food neophobia**, i.e. avoidance of novel foods and
- **food technology neophobia**, i.e. fear or distrust of new food production technologies," [^7]
- "greater **curiosity and openness** "- good [^8]
- Consumer **awareness and openness** to new food experiences [^8]

#### Produktfaktoren
- "One of the most prominent barriers identified is the perceived **lack of naturalness** of cultured meat." [^1]
- **Gesundheitsbedenken** "Perceptions of naturalness are closely linked to concerns about health" [^1]
	- Kann jedoch auch Gesundheitsfördernd wahrgenommen werden; z.B. durch das Ergänzen des Nährwertprofils [^2] 
- "**sensory** perceptions, particularly taste" [^2]
- **Terminologie und Verpackung** [^2]
- **Formatvertrautheit** [^2]

#### Externe Faktoren
- "**environmental impact** and 
- **animal welfare** concerns are primary motivators" - good [^3]
- "impact **local agricultural** industries." - bad[^3]
- **Social influences** also play a critical role in shaping consumer acceptance. [^3]
- **Social Media** (besonders Micro-Influencer) [^4]
- **Produktpräsentation** und Kommunikation [^5]
	- auch negative Aspekte von herkömmlichen Fleisch aufzuzählen
	- "presenting cultured meat as “real meat”" - good
	- "technical framings, such as detailed descriptions of the production process, tend to evoke negative perceptions related to artificiality and unnaturalness"
In conclusion, external factors such as **societal benefits, social influences and the framing of information** play a pivotal role in the acceptance of cultured meat


"Research has also highlighted the importance of external influences, including media framing, trust in institutions and broader societal narratives about food innovation. "[^6]
